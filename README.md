# ANDAHA — Parsamina & Luxury Textile Business Portal

An immersive, single-page 3D portal experience for **ANDAHA Luxury Textiles & Family Fashion**.

Features a video preloader sequence with dynamic voiceover narration subtitles, interactive 3D HTML5 Canvas portal with focal perspective projection & tilt physics, 3D flying silk ribbon particle simulation, and luxury fashion collections.

## Collections Showcase

- **Timeless Tradition**: Kanchipuram & Banarasi Zari Silk Sarees, 100% pure Mulberry silk & real gold threads.
- **Modern Style**: Contemporary women's executive couture, Italian wool blend & pure silk satin.
- **Contemporary Men**: Super 150s Merino wool blazers & urban jackets.
- **Little Ones**: 100% Organic Egyptian cotton & soft muslin family kids' fashion.

## Run Locally

```bash
python -m http.server 8000
```

Then open `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.

## Deployment

Deployable instantly on GitHub Pages, Vercel, or Netlify with zero build step required.
